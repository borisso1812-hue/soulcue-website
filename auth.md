# Authentication for SoulCue (soulcue.org)

## Overview

SoulCue (soulcue.org) is a public content website. All pages are publicly accessible without authentication.

## Public Access

- All articles and guides: publicly accessible, no authentication required
- Sitemap: https://soulcue.org/sitemap.xml
- Content index for AI agents: https://soulcue.org/llms.txt

## Content Usage Policy

- **search**: yes — content may be indexed for search
- **ai-input**: yes — content may be used as input for AI-generated responses
- **ai-train**: no — content may not be used for training AI models

## iOS App

SoulCue iOS app is available at:
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/soulcue-ai-growth-coach/id6754840010

Authentication for the iOS app is handled within the app and is not exposed via this website.

## Contact

For questions about content usage or agent access, the site is operated by the SoulCue team.
